MAYA Prompting Engine

Fixed, baked into every render (not editable here)

Every MAYA image combines four layers: the design specification from the consultation, the client's body, the client's face, and the framing mandate below. Your four fields add atelier directions.

FRAMING, NON NEGOTIABLE: one continuous horizontal photograph of the SAME model standing THREE times across a single seamless studio set, SIDE view on the left, FRONT view in the center (dominant hero), BACK view on the right. ONE unbroken backdrop and ONE continuous floor run edge to edge behind and beneath all three figures. NO panel dividers, NO seams, NO borders, NO collage or grid look. FULL BODY for each figure, head to soles inside the frame. Same model, same pose category, same height for all three. Plain clean seamless studio backdrop, soft even lighting, fashion editorial photography. No text, no labels, no captions, no measurement marks.

The body layer: when measurements are captured in the Profile fold, the prompt adds "The model has these proportions: approximately H inches tall, S inch shoulders, C inch chest, W inch waist, H inch hips." Since v14.20 every render also carries, measurements or not: "Natural adult photographic proportions: the head is about one eighth of the standing height and is never enlarged, the neck, shoulders, torso and legs are in realistic scale to it, and the face reference sets likeness only, never the size of the head." If a measurement was never typed in, it is not sent, and the image model guesses; image models treat height as a weak suggestion unless the words reinforce it. The Body field below is appended right after that sentence as "BODY, atelier direction", so this is where "long legs, elongated proportions, a small head" goes.

The face layer: gender presentation, age range, skin tone, face shape, eyes and hair are extracted from the face photo once and repeated verbatim to keep the person consistent across views.

Your four fields: stored in Firestore, read at sign in, and added to every render as CHARACTER, BACKGROUND, STYLE and BODY atelier directions.
