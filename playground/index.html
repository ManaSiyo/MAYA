What MAYA is

MAYA is a design tool by Mana Siyo. You describe a garment, MAYA renders visions of it, and if you submit a design, Mana Siyo can make the real garment. By using MAYA you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

Your account

You sign in with a Google account and you are responsible for what happens under it. You must be 16 or older. We can suspend an account that breaks these terms or abuses the service.

Your content

What you make and upload stays yours. You give Mana Siyo the permission needed to store it, process it, show it back to you, and produce the garment you ask for. You must have the right to anything you upload, and the permission of any real person whose photograph you attach.

Built on artificial intelligence

MAYA is an AI system, and using it means interacting with one. The renders, the design writing, the transcripts of what you say and the summaries you read are produced by AI models from OpenAI and Google, running under Mana Siyo's own accounts. Every garment picture MAYA shows you is AI generated unless it is a photograph you uploaded yourself, and illustrative fabric pictures are labeled Generated. Generated pictures carry the machine readable provenance marks their models embed. AI can be wrong: renders are visualizations, not promises of the finished garment.

Your face photograph

A face photograph you attach is used for one thing only: rendering the garment on that person, for you. It goes to our AI providers only to make the picture you asked for. MAYA does not run facial recognition, keeps no scan of face geometry or other biometric identifier, never sells or shares the photograph, and never publishes it. Replace or remove it, or delete your account, and it is deleted. You must have the permission of any real person whose photograph you attach.

The community wall

This is the one rule to actually read. When you heart a garment MAYA generated, that picture is published to the community wall, where every signed in MAYA user can see it, with your first name and the garment's one line description. Take the heart away and the post comes down. Only garments MAYA rendered are ever published: your uploaded pictures, fabric photographs and face photographs never appear on the wall.

Renders and orders

MAYA's pictures are AI generated visualizations, not photographs and not promises: the sewn garment is made by people and can differ from a render. Submitting a design starts a conversation with the atelier; prices, fit sessions and delivery are agreed there, not in the app. Tips are voluntary and processed by Stripe.
