MAYA Prompting Engine

Fixed, baked into every render (not editable here)

Every image MAYA generates is built from four layers: the design specification from the consultation, the client's body, the client's face, and the framing mandate below. Your three fields further down are appended on top as atelier directions.

FRAMING, NON NEGOTIABLE: one continuous horizontal photograph of the SAME model standing THREE times across a single seamless studio set, SIDE view on the left, FRONT view in the center (dominant hero), BACK view on the right. ONE unbroken backdrop and ONE continuous floor run edge to edge behind and beneath all three figures. NO panel dividers, NO seams, NO borders, NO collage or grid look. FULL BODY for each figure, head to soles inside the frame. Same model, same pose category, same height for all three. Plain clean seamless studio backdrop, soft even lighting, fashion editorial photography. No text, no labels, no captions, no measurement marks.

The body layer: when measurements are captured in the avatar drawer, the prompt adds "The model has these proportions: approximately H inches tall, S inch shoulders, C inch chest, W inch waist, H inch hips." If a measurement was never typed in, it is not sent, and the image model guesses. That is why a 6 foot 6 character can come out short: image models treat height as a weak suggestion unless the rest of the prompt reinforces it. Writing something like "an unusually tall model, long legs, elongated proportions" in The Character field below pushes it much harder than the number alone.

The face layer: gender presentation, age range, skin tone, face shape, eyes and hair are extracted once from the face photo and repeated verbatim in every render so the person stays the same across views.

Your three fields: saved here, stored in Firestore, read by the live app at sign in, and appended to every render as "CHARACTER atelier direction", "BACKGROUND atelier direction", "STYLE atelier direction".

Recent changes, what has shipped
Aug 11Saving is fixed. There is one save path, it goes to the cloud, and it only says Saved when it truly saved. Naming a project used to keep it in the browser alone, which is how a client could be saved and then not be there.
Aug 11New really means new project. It closes the one you are in first, so nothing already saved is ever written over. Same when you switch client.
Aug 11Nothing is kept on the Mac any more. Projects, pictures and the client roster all live online, so any device shows the same work and a refresh brings the last project back.
Aug 11The API keys left the browser. The Brief and the Operations Room call MAYA's own server, which holds them; your Google sign in is what authorises the call.
Aug 11Faster: submissions upload together instead of one after another, the project list reads a small index, and new submissions appear here within seconds.
Aug 11The self destruct link is gone, a stranger could send a client a link that erased their whole account. Uploads are limited to the files MAYA itself writes, and the Firestore and Storage rules now publish with every deploy.
Aug 6Each submission is dissected once and the pieces are saved to its Drive folder, reopening a client costs no API credits.
Aug 6Operations Room is the new name, in the Brief, on this map, and in the beta.
Aug 6Systems Map slimmed: one line doors, lights in the top bar, submissions flow edge to edge, prompting engine and this list fold away.
Aug 5One push publishing: Push origin in GitHub Desktop deploys the website and the server together, about 4 minutes.
Aug 5Clicking a submission opens the Brief with biography, vision and measurements filled, then it dissects itself into pieces that feed the Operations Room.
Aug 5Systems Map redesigned to the MAYA look, one sign in unlocks submissions, traffic and prompting.
Aug 5Google Drive key renewed, client submissions land again.
Aug 5Folder reorganized: three pages at the root, aesthetics for everything visual, docs for everything else.
