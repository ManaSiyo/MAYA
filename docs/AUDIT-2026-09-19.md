# September 19 audit: economics, voice and direct marketing data

Status: investigation and proposed release order, not a shipped milestone. No push,
credential changes, billing changes, production configuration changes or carrier
migration performed. Existing September 19 CRM changes are in commit 9b0beac;
their regression validation remains outstanding. The last verified release label
is v14.36. This is a focused audit, not a completed whole-repository certification.

## Live incident

Admin currently renders a Windsor account-limit warning as two campaign names,
with zero spend and NO DELIVERY. The warning says five accounts are connected
while the free plan permits one and reporting reads are paused. The generated
ticker incorrectly concludes that paid campaigns are paused due to account limits.
Reporting unavailable must mean unknown, never zero delivery or paused campaigns.
Actual campaign delivery needs independent verification in the source platforms.

## Existing direct integrations

server.js metaInsights and googleAdsInsights already fetch account aggregates
directly. GA4 reporting already uses analyticsdata.googleapis.com. However,
adCombined daily charts and campaign tables are constructed inside the Windsor
connected branch. Direct credentials alone therefore do not replace Windsor.
Google Ads currently calls v18 and checks for a developer token; review current
API version and access requirements before activation. Explicitly bind the correct
GA4 property rather than relying on marketingProperty's first-property fallback.

Build platform adapters with one normalized account/campaign/day schema, durable
cached snapshots, independent source status, last successful sync, error handling
and pagination. Then switch charts and warnings to that schema. Keep Windsor
until direct reports reconcile; Fromsa handles access and any cancellation.

The current costPerLead divides total ad spend by all form leads; it is blended,
not campaign attribution. Capture campaign IDs/UTMs and eligible click identifiers
at lead creation, preserve attribution through booking and payment, and show
unattributed leads explicitly. Never infer a person's campaign from aggregate data.

## Voice

OpenAI generates speech; Twilio transports the phone call. The current Media
Streams path uses 8 kHz mu-law, a fidelity limitation documented by Twilio.
Changing carriers alone does not guarantee clearer ordinary phone calls.
Prioritize a natural, concise owner-specific prompt, measured turn latency,
faster grounded CRM tools, interruption/playback synchronization and startup
latency. Compare voice samples in browser WebRTC and by telephone before choosing.
Browser voice can avoid the telephone audio path. Evaluate direct OpenAI SIP
with a carrier as a later transport option, not a promised quality improvement.

## Economics findings

The internal meter is an estimate, not a bill: it charges a flat $0.10 per
Realtime session, regardless of length, and flat chat/image allowances. Some
automatic lead-summary calls bypass noteSpend. Realtime usage is not reconciled
from response usage. Therefore actual consumed credits and remaining balance
cannot be established from that meter. OpenAI and Twilio dashboards require owner
sign-in before actual spend can be verified. No secrets inspected.

OpenAI usage includes image generation/editing, vision and text analysis, prompt
rewrites, voice input/output and context, transcription, lead summaries, drafts
and marketing briefs. CRM lookups themselves need no model call. Twilio number,
minutes and SMS are separate; Google hosting/storage and ad spend are separate.

First release should add a usage ledger by provider, model, feature, request and
call with token counts/duration, a dated pricing table and invoice reconciliation.
Keep measured usage, estimated dollars, app quotas and provider balances separate.

## Proposed order

1. Correct misleading unavailable marketing data and complete CRM lookup/scrollbar
   regression verification; establish actual provider spend.
2. Improve voice latency and speaking style with measured browser/phone comparisons.
3. Finish direct GA4, Google Ads and Meta dashboards; reconcile then retire Windsor.
4. Durable new-lead events: immediate owner SMS with callback number plus call,
   duplicate prevention, delivery status and bounded unanswered-call retry.
5. Active CRM: structured next action/due date, unified communication timeline,
   consultation/design/deposit/fitting/production/delivery stages and overdue alerts.
6. Link campaign to lead to paid order; show response time, conversion, collected
   revenue and marketing return. Maya cites the underlying records and timestamps.
