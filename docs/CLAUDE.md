# Working preferences (Fromsa · Mana Siyo)

## Response format — ALWAYS
- End every response with a short **Summary** at the bottom.
- The summary must be a **max ~20-second read**: under four sentences, never more than four short paragraphs.
- Keep it concise on purpose — full detail goes above; the summary is the skim layer.
- Apply this to all responses, across all chats.
