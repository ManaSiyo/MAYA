Your content

Built on artificial intelligence

MAYA is an AI system, and using it means interacting with one. The renders, the design writing, the transcripts of what you say and the summaries you read are produced by artificial intelligence models from OpenAI and Google, running under Mana Siyo's own accounts. Every garment picture MAYA shows you is AI generated unless it is a photograph you uploaded yourself, and illustrative fabric pictures are labeled Generated. Generated pictures carry the machine readable provenance marks their models embed, so they can be recognized as AI made.

AI can be wrong. Renders are visualizations, not measurements, medical or fit advice, or promises of the finished garment, and you should not rely on them as anything else. This disclosure is made in the spirit of the transparency rules for AI systems, including the EU AI Act and California's AI transparency law.

Your face photograph

If you attach a photograph of a face, MAYA uses it for exactly one thing: rendering the garment on that person, for you. It is sent to our AI providers only to produce the picture you asked for. MAYA does not run facial recognition, does not build or keep any scan of face geometry or other biometric identifier, does not use the photograph to identify anyone, and never sells or shares it for advertising. It never appears on the community wall. Replace or remove the photograph, or delete your account, and it is deleted; a person in a photograph can also write to us and have it removed. You must have the permission of any real person whose photograph you attach, and you may not use MAYA to depict a real person deceptively.

The community wall

This is the one rule to actually read. When you heart a garment MAYA generated, that picture is published to the community wall, where every signed in MAYA user can see it, with your first name and the garment's one line description. Take the heart away and the post comes down. Only garments MAYA rendered are ever published: your uploaded pictures, fabric photographs and face photographs never appear on the wall.

Renders and orders

MAYA's pictures are AI generated visualizations, not photographs and not promises: the sewn garment is made by people and can differ from a render. Submitting a design starts a conversation with the atelier; prices, fit sessions and delivery are agreed there, not in the app. Tips are voluntary and processed by Stripe.
