MAYA Prompting Engine

Fixed, baked into every render (not editable here)

Every image MAYA generates is built from four layers: the design specification from the consultation, the client's body, the client's face, and the framing mandate below. Your three fields further down are appended on top as atelier directions.

FRAMING, NON NEGOTIABLE: one continuous horizontal photograph of the SAME model standing THREE times across a single seamless studio set, SIDE view on the left, FRONT view in the center (dominant hero), BACK view on the right. ONE unbroken backdrop and ONE continuous floor run edge to edge behind and beneath all three figures. NO panel dividers, NO seams, NO borders, NO collage or grid look. FULL BODY for each figure, head to soles inside the frame. Same model, same pose category, same height for all three. Plain clean seamless studio backdrop, soft even lighting, fashion editorial photography. No text, no labels, no captions, no measurement marks.

The body layer: when measurements are captured in the avatar drawer, the prompt adds "The model has these proportions: approximately H inches tall, S inch shoulders, C inch chest, W inch waist, H inch hips." If a measurement was never typed in, it is not sent, and the image model guesses. That is why a 6 foot 6 character can come out short: image models treat height as a weak suggestion unless the rest of the prompt reinforces it. Writing something like "an unusually tall model, long legs, elongated proportions" in The Character field below pushes it much harder than the number alone.

The face layer: gender presentation, age range, skin tone, face shape, eyes and hair are extracted once from the face photo and repeated verbatim in every render so the person stays the same across views.

Your three fields: saved here, stored in Firestore, read by the live app at sign in, and appended to every render as "CHARACTER atelier direction", "BACKGROUND atelier direction", "STYLE atelier direction".
